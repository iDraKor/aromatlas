What the application does

Aromatlas Reels is an internal tool of Aromatlas. It uploads videos made by Aromatlas to the Aromatlas TikTok account as drafts, using the TikTok Content Posting API. The account owner reviews each draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to publish it.

Who may use it

The application is not offered to the public. Only the Aromatlas team uses it, with its own TikTok account and after authorizing it through TikTok's official login.

Content

All videos sent by the application are created and owned by Aromatlas. Aromatlas is responsible for their compliance with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Access and withdrawal

The authorization can be withdrawn at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services). The application then stops working.

The application is provided as is, for internal use, without any warranty.
