Aromatlas

Taste the world, cup by cup. The Coffee Atlas, one origin at a time.

Aromatlas tells the story of a coffee origin in each short video, as a card of a collectible series, on Instagram (@aromatlas.co) and TikTok.

The Aromatlas Reels application is a private tool used only by the Aromatlas team to send its own videos, as drafts, to its own TikTok account.
