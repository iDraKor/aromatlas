Privacy Policy

Aromatlas Reels · last updated 5 November 2026

Summary

Aromatlas Reels does not collect any data about the public or about other TikTok users. It only acts on the Aromatlas TikTok account, for the Aromatlas team.

Data the application handles

When the Aromatlas account authorizes the application, TikTok returns an access token, a refresh token, the account's open ID and its display name. They are used only to upload Aromatlas videos as drafts and to check which account is connected.

Where it is stored

These tokens are stored only on the Aromatlas team's own computer. They are never sold, shared or sent to any third party, and no analytics or advertising tools are used.

Retention and deletion

The tokens are kept while the authorization is active. Withdrawing the authorization in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services) makes them unusable, and they are then deleted from the computer.

Questions can be sent to Aromatlas through Instagram: @aromatlas.co.
